# Contributing

Sara v2 has four planes: Governance & Delivery, Context & Cognition, Runtime & Capability, and Evidence & Assurance.

A contribution should improve one plane without creating a competing authority in another.

Good contributions include clearer templates, smaller Agent responsibilities, reusable Skills, better context contracts, stronger evidence/rollback rules, reproducible evaluations, sanitized examples, and validation improvements.

Discuss first before adding a standing Agent, always-on service/database, write-capable automation, authority changes, self-promoting memory, major licensing changes, or major terminology changes.

Every material proposal should state the bottleneck, simpler baseline, owning plane, duplication risk, acceptance evidence, and reversibility.

Do not publish credentials, customer material, private project identifiers, internal machine paths, or raw private execution evidence.
